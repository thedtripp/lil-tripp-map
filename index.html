Lil' Tripp

Find tech jobs you can actually get to.

A map of tech companies and their careers pages, so your search starts from where you live, not from a list of cities.

Open the live demo I'd use this

Why it exists

Job boards sort by title and keyword. They don't care that a role means a 90-minute commute each way. If a long commute is what makes you hate a job, you shouldn't have to open forty tabs to find out where the company is.

Lil' Tripp flips the search: start with the map, see who's nearby, then go to the careers page.

What the demo does today

Map of companiesTech companies pinned at their actual office address.
Search and filterFind a company or street. Show only those with a careers page.
Straight to careersEach pin links to the company's website and careers page, where we have one.

Where it could go

These are ideas, not features. Your interest tells me which matter:

  • Commute radius: show only companies within a walk, bike ride or transit time of your address.
  • More cities: the demo covers one part of San Francisco.
  • Open roles: show how many engineering roles a company has open.
  • Other uses: recruiters mapping outreach, founders scouting where talent clusters, people choosing a neighborhood to live in.
